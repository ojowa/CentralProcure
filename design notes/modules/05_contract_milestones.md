# Proposal 5: Contract Lifecycle & Milestone Tracking

## 1. Goal
Transition approved awards into legally binding contracts and monitor their physical and financial progress.

## 2. PPA 2007 Control Requirements
- **Contract Linkage:** Every contract must be linked to a valid **Award Code**.
- **Performance Security:** Track the submission and validity of the mandatory Performance Bond.
- **Progress Verification:** Payments must be blocked unless the required **Milestone (Progress %)** is achieved and verified.

## 3. UI Flow
- **Contract Ledger:** List all active and completed contracts.
- **Milestone Tracker:** Allow Contract Managers to log progress updates and upload inspection photos.
- **Timeline View:** A Gantt-style view showing the start, current progress, and end dates.
