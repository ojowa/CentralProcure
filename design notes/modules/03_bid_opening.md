# Proposal 3: Bid Opening Session Control

## 1. Goal
Ensure transparency by providing a time-locked, official interface for the public opening of bids.

## 2. PPA 2007 Control Requirements
- **Time Integrity:** Bids cannot be accessed until the exact closing time (Section 27).
- **Official Witnessing:** Generate a system-timestamped "Bid Opening Record" for all observers.
- **Presence Log:** Record representatives present (NGOs, Professional Bodies, Vendors).

## 3. UI Flow
- **Opening Terminal:** A "Big Screen" view that counts down to the closing time.
- **Unlocking Action:** An official (e.g., Tenders Board Secretary) clicks the "Unlock Bids" button.
- **Record Export:** Immediate download of the PDF summary for manual signing and public display.
