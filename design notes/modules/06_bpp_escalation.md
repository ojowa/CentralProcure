# Proposal 6: BPP "No Objection" Escalation Workspace

## 1. Goal
Automate the submission of high-value procurement files to the Bureau of Public Procurement (BPP) for federal oversight.

## 2. PPA 2007 Control Requirements
- **Mandatory Prior Review:** Tenders exceeding specified thresholds CANNOT proceed to award without a BPP Certificate of "No Objection."
- **Document Packager:** Automatically bundle all mandatory documents (APP, Requisition, Ad, Evaluation, etc.).
- **Decision Tracking:** Log BPP's feedback, approval code, and date.

## 3. UI Flow
- **Escalation Queue:** BPP Liaison view showing cases pending external review.
- **Packaging View:** Review and confirm all required PDF attachments are present.
- **Status Dashboard:** Track "In Review" cases and capture the final "No Objection" certificate.
