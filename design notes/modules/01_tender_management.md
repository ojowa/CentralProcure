# Proposal 1: Tender Management Workspace (PPA 2007)

## 1. Goal
Provide Procurement Officers a secure UI to convert an approved **Requisition** into a **Live Tender (Advertisement)**.

## 2. PPA 2007 Control Requirements
- **Mandatory Ad Record:** Title, Category, and Budget Code must be pre-populated from the APP/Requisition.
- **Timeline Logic:** Closing date must be at least 6 weeks (42 days) from publication for National Competitive Bidding (NCB).
- **Eligibility Criteria:** Upload mandatory requirements (e.g., CAC, PENCOM, ITF, NSITF, BPP IRR).

## 3. UI Flow
- **Dashboard:** List all tenders (Draft, Published, Closed).
- **Ad Form:** Capture bidding deadlines, location, and technical/financial submission requirements.
- **Publication Control:** A final "Confirm & Publish" step that locks the tender and makes it visible on the Vendor Portal.
