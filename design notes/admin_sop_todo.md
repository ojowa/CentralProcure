# Admin SOP Notes

## Module Access: Restrict Legal From Planning Committee

Use this when you want to block legal users from the Planning Committee module.

1. Open `User and Role Management` in the internal admin dashboard.
2. Go to `Module Access`.
3. Select `By Role`.
4. In `Step 1: Select Role`, choose `LegalReviewer` (or your legal role name).
5. Search for `Planning Committee`.
6. On `Planning Committee Review` (`procurement-planning-committee`), click `Block Access`.
7. Refresh and verify with a legal user account.

Revert later:
- Click `Use Default` on that same module for the legal role.
