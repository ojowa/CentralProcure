# Appendix F: UI Wireframe List & System Sitemap
## Electronic Procurement System – Nigeria Immigration Service (NIS)

---

## Purpose

This appendix presents a **structured wireframe list and sitemap** for the Electronic Procurement System.

It shows:
- How users navigate the system
- Logical grouping of screens by role
- Clear separation between internal and external access

This document is suitable for **design, development, and stakeholder validation**.

## 3. Wireframe Design Notes

- All dashboards are **role-driven**
- No user sees screens outside their mandate
- Approval screens are read-only until action is permitted
- Audit screens are strictly non-editable

---

## 4. Governance Value

The sitemap and wireframe structure:
- Reinforces separation of duties
- Prevents unauthorised navigation
- Supports secure, intuitive workflows
- Simplifies user training and adoption
